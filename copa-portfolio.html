Copa Mattress and Furniture Website

Developed a responsive, modern e-commerce storefront for Copa Mattress and Furniture, a local mattress and furniture retailer in Maricopa, Arizona, using ReactJS with Vite. The site features a clean charcoal-and-gold design, intuitive navigation, per-size product pricing, and e-commerce-ready components to enhance the shopping experience.
