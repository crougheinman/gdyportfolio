Entity Duel

Entity Duel is a browser-based, Magic-style trading card game built with React and Vite. Two duelists race to bring each other from 20 life to 0 by playing lands for mana, casting creatures, sorceries and instants, and trading blows across clearly defined turn phases.

It ships with three ways to play: a single-player mode against a rule-driven AI opponent, a local Hotseat mode for two players on one device, and real-time Online PvP powered by Supabase. Players choose from six pre-built decks, each with its own archetype and identity - from Emberwood aggro to Stonewall control.

Technical Features

Frontend

  • React - component-based, reactive game UI
  • Vite - fast build and HMR dev server
  • Zustand - lightweight, central game-state store
  • Hand-crafted CSS - dark, fantasy card-game theme (Cinzel & Grenze Gotisch)
  • Responsive Design - plays on desktop and mobile

Engine & Services

  • Rules Engine - mana, card types, combat and keyword resolution
  • AI Opponent - rule-driven computer player
  • Supabase - real-time Online PvP and data persistence
  • Netlify - static hosting and CI deploys

Gameplay Features

  • Three modes: Play vs AI, Hotseat (2 players, 1 device) and Online PvP
  • Six decks: Aggro, Control, Spellslinger, Swarm, Ramp and Defensive archetypes
  • Mana system: lands tap for mana, one land per turn
  • Card types: Land, Creature, Sorcery and Instant
  • Combat: attackers, blockers and keywords such as Haste
  • Turn phases: Main / Combat / End, with an in-app rulebook and activity log

Development Approach

Entity Duel showcases interactive front-end engineering: a deterministic rules engine drives every card interaction, global game state is managed with Zustand, and Supabase provides real-time synchronisation for online matches. The AI opponent evaluates the board each turn to make its plays, and the whole experience is delivered as a fast, responsive single-page app.
