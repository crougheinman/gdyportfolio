Smith Group Admin

Smith Group Admin is the multi-tenant back office behind a group of mattress and furniture retailers. One CodeIgniter 4 application serves several stores, each with its own orders, staff, delivery zones and website, and the same app exposes the REST API that every store's storefront calls.

Staff scan paper invoices and the system turns them into orders, then carries each order through payment, delivery scheduling and customer messaging. Sales reps, managers, delivery crews and warehouse workers each see only the screens their role needs.

  • CodeIgniter 4
  • PHP
  • MySQL
  • JavaScript
  • Docker

Technical Features

Back End

  • CodeIgniter 4 / PHP 8 - explicit routing, thin controllers
  • Service layer - business rules kept out of controllers
  • MySQL - migrations, soft deletes and entities
  • Three access layers - session login for staff, token auth for the API, per-store keys for public storefronts
  • Queued email - sent by a CLI worker, not inline

Integrations

  • Twilio - SMS and WhatsApp to customers
  • PrintNode - fax and print to each store's machine
  • AI document reading - Gemini extracts order details from scanned invoices
  • Network scanners - a small per-store agent feeds scans in
  • Telegram - live alerts for new activity and inquiries

What It Does

  • Orders from scans: scan an invoice, review the extracted draft, confirm the sale
  • Payments & status: record payments and move each order through a five-step tracker
  • Deliveries & routes: 14-day capacity by zone, daily stop lists and an approval step
  • Message Centre: automatic order texts plus a chat view for SMS and WhatsApp replies
  • Sales floor mode: a kiosk screen for reps to log sales and see their standing
  • Roles: admin, manager, sales, delivery and warehouse accounts, each locked to its own screens

Development Approach

I built it as one application with clear boundaries: controllers only orchestrate, services hold the business rules, and custom validators guard every form. Routes are listed explicitly so nothing is exposed by accident, and an activity log records who did what for every important change. Long-running work such as email, fax and scanning runs through queues and CLI workers so the screens stay fast.

Screenshots use demo data. Customer names, contact details, addresses, scanned documents, staff photos and financial totals are blurred.
